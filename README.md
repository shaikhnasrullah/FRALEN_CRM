## 💎 About Fralen CRM

**Fralen CRM** is a web-based customer relationship management solution created for optical shops. It helps shop owners organize customer information, maintain prescription records, manage orders, and handle follow-ups in a more structured way.

Our goal is to make everyday optical business operations simpler, more organized, and more efficient through technology.
